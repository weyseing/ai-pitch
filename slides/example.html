AI Workshop
05 / 21
Concept · 1 of 2

AI agent = the brain.

  • Understands plain language & messy input
  • Decides the next step; can call tools
  • Handles things it has never seen before
  • Flexible — but not 100% predictable
The model · its brainReasoning core (LLM)understands your words & decides what to do
equipped with
Toolssearch · email · sheets · your apps
Memorythe chat so far · your files

AI Workshop
06 / 21
Concept · 2 of 2

RPA bot = the hands.

  • Follows exact, pre-set steps
  • Runs on a trigger or on demand
  • Predictable, repeatable, tireless
  • No understanding — change the page, it breaks
Best for · repetitive, structured tasks
Step 1→Step 2→Done
open booking page fill name, email, slot click "Confirm" repeat every row — no human
